About the team

We are a student robotics team competing in the FIRST Tech Challenge. Each season we design, build, program and drive a robot to play a new game against teams from across the state.

We are based in North Branch, Michigan and have competed since 2013, with the support of Ruth Fox Middle School.

Our name is our approach: reuse parts, repair what breaks, and learn how things work by taking them apart.
